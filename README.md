A secure, full-stack financial management and savings tracking portal built specifically for Chandu.

- **Default Password**: `chandu@2026`
- **Security**: Password hashed using `node:crypto` `scryptSync`, server-side HMAC authorization tokens, and 401 gatekeeping on all `/api/financial/*` endpoints. Private financial data is hidden from public portfolio visitors.

### 🎯 Savings Goal Configuration
- **Goal Name**: `My First ₹1 Lakh Savings`
- **Target Amount**: `₹100,000`
- **Starting Balance**: `₹0`
- **Target Duration**: `3 Months`
- **Planned Monthly Contribution**: `₹5,000`
- **Required Monthly Rate**: `~₹33,333/month` (to hit ₹1 Lakh in 3 months)
- **3-Month Plan Shortfall**: `₹85,000` (at ₹5,000/month, ₹15,000 will be saved by deadline, falling short by ₹85,000)
- **Projected Completion**: Dynamically calculated at current savings pace (~20 months at ₹5,000/mo)
- **Assumptions**: Transparently stated on the widget. Can be edited at any time via the "Edit Goal & Plan" modal.

### 📊 8 Overview Cards
1. **Total Freelance Received**: Cleared client payouts only (`status === 'received'`).
2. **Pending Client Payments**: Invoiced client payments awaiting clearance (`status === 'pending'`).
3. **Business Expenses**: Deductible business costs (software, gear, sound libraries).
4. **Personal Expenses**: Living & lifestyle costs tracked separately.
5. **Net Business Profit**: `Total Freelance Received − Business Expenses`.
6. **Actual Savings Balance**: `Starting Balance + Contributions In − Withdrawals`.
7. **Target Amount Remaining**: `Target Amount − Actual Savings Balance`.
8. **Goal Completion Percentage**: Dynamic progress towards ₹1 Lakh.

### 🛡️ Savings Transactions & Overdraft Guard
- Form to transfer money into savings (`deposit`) or withdraw from savings (`withdrawal`).
- **Overdraft Guard**: Prevents any withdrawal that exceeds the available savings balance.
- Full edit and delete controls.

### 📑 Income & Expense Separation
- Invoices do not count towards business income or profit until marked as **"Received"**.
- Savings transfers are neither counted as new expenses nor new business income.
- Business profit is kept strictly separate from personal cash flow.

## ⭐ Client Rating & Feedback System

A complete, end-to-end client review system with cryptographic tokens, single-use security, rating breakdowns, and private moderation.

### 1. Generating a Client Feedback Link
1. Go to your private admin dashboard at `http://localhost:5173/#admin` (or click **Client Reviews** in the dashboard navigation).
2. Log in with your admin credentials.
3. Switch to the **"Client Feedback"** tab.
4. Click **"+ Create New Client Link"**.
5. Fill in the details:
   - **Client Name**: e.g., *"Rajesh Sharma"*
   - **Project Title / Deliverable**: e.g., *"YouTube Shorts Series (5 Videos)"*
   - **Client Email / WhatsApp Number**: (Optional, kept strictly private)
   - **Expiry Window**: 7 days, 14 days, 30 days, or 60 days.
6. Click **"Generate Secure Feedback Link"**.

### 2. Sending the Link to Your Client
Once created, a secure, difficult-to-guess cryptographic token link is generated:
- **Direct Link**: `http://localhost:5173/#feedback?token=...`
- **1-Click WhatsApp Share**: Click the green **"WhatsApp"** button to open a pre-formatted delivery message directly in WhatsApp with the project title and feedback link.
- **Copy Link**: Click the copy icon to paste into email, Instagram DM, or Telegram.

### 3. Client Submission Experience
- The link takes the client to a dedicated, responsive feedback page designed for mobile and desktop.
- Displays project details and client name.
- **1 to 5 Stars**:
  - ⭐ **Overall Experience**
  - 🎬 **Video Editing Quality**
  - 💬 **Communication & Responsiveness**
  - ⏱️ **Delivery Speed & Deadlines**
- **Written Review**: Optional text box for detailed feedback.
- **Public Consent Checkbox**: Explicit permission toggle (*"Allow Edit Revolution to display my review publicly on the portfolio"*).
- **Anti-Tampering & Anti-Duplicate**: Tokens are cryptographically generated (48 hex characters) and expire after single use. Once submitted, the link is permanently locked against further submissions.

### 4. Admin Review & Moderation Workflow
- **Overview Analytics**: Real-time stats on total reviews, average overall rating, category averages (Quality, Comms, Delivery), consent rate, and automated improvement area flags.
- **Moderation Actions**:
  - **Approve for Publication**: Makes the review visible in the public portfolio Testimonials section (strictly requires that the client also opted in for public display).
  - **Reject / Archive**: Keeps feedback archived in your records without publishing.
  - **Private Response / Notes**: Log follow-up actions and private notes.
  - **Delete**: Permanently removes spam or invalid submissions.

### 5. Genuine Public Testimonials
- The public portfolio **"Client Ratings & Reviews"** section displays only verified reviews that:
  1. Were genuinely submitted through a unique project link.
  2. Have explicit client consent (`allow_public_display = 1`).
  3. Were approved by you in the dashboard (`status = 'approved'`).
- Client email, phone number, and internal follow-up notes are strictly hidden from the public API.
